STEP-BY-STEP INSTALLATION GUIDE

  1. Surface cleaning: Thoroughly clean the optical sensor area on the back of your smartwatch using isopropyl alcohol or an alcohol wipe. Ensure it is 100% dry, clean, and free from oils or dust.
  2. Alignment: Carefully remove the protective film from the adhesive side of the Sensor TattooFix® lens and align it centrally over the optical sensor.
  3. Application: Press the lens firmly against the sensor for 30 to 45 seconds using uniform pressure to eliminate micro air bubbles and activate the adhesive bond.
