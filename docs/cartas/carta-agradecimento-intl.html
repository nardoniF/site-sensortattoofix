Step-by-step installation guide
  1. 1
    Surface cleaning: Thoroughly clean the optical sensor area on the back of your smartwatch using isopropyl alcohol or an alcohol wipe. Ensure it is 100% dry, clean, and free from oils or dust.
  2. 2
    Alignment: Carefully remove the protective film from the adhesive side of the Sensor TattooFix® lens and align it centrally over the optical sensor.
  3. 3
    Application: Press the lens firmly against the sensor for 30 to 45 seconds using uniform pressure to eliminate micro air bubbles and activate the adhesive bond.
Adhesive curing schedule (crucial for maximum longevity)
4 Hours Regular use Safe for casual daily wear.
48 Hours Workouts / sweat Required before heavy sweat or exercise.
72 Hours Water exposure Full cure required for swimming or showers.
Important usage notes
  • Wireless charging: Your Sensor TattooFix® lens is fully compatible with original OEM magnetic chargers. You do not need to remove the lens to charge.
