As requested, we are including this second optical lens for your Whoop 5 MG smartband — packed in the same shipment as your Apple Watch lens.

This compact lens was custom-made for the smaller sensor on fitness bands and wearables. Please follow the same installation and curing steps below for best adhesion and sensor performance.

Step-by-step installation guide
  1. 1
    Surface cleaning: Thoroughly clean the optical sensor on the back of your Whoop band using isopropyl alcohol or an alcohol wipe. Ensure it is 100% dry, clean, and free from oils or dust.
  2. 2
    Alignment: Remove the protective film from the adhesive side and center the lens over the optical sensor (this lens is smaller than our smartwatch lenses).
  3. 3
    Application: Press firmly for 30 to 45 seconds with uniform pressure to eliminate micro air bubbles and activate the adhesive bond.
Adhesive curing schedule (crucial for maximum longevity)
4 Hours Regular use Safe for casual daily wear.
48 Hours Workouts / sweat Required before heavy sweat or exercise.
72 Hours Water exposure Full cure required for swimming or showers.
Important usage notes
  • Charging: The lens is fully compatible with your Whoop battery pack — you do not need to remove the lens when charging.
  • Following the curing timeline above guarantees long-lasting adhesion even during high-intensity training.
