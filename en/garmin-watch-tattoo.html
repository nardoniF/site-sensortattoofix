Garmin on a tattooed wrist: heart rate and sleep gaps

Garmin is great for training, but it still uses optical LEDs. Dark ink under the watch means less light back — and gappy data.

Typical Garmin symptoms

  • Heart-rate charts full of holes or spikes
  • Sleep / Body Battery / stress looking off
  • Workouts that pause or lose pulse at the gym

Why

Even brands that “handle tattoos better” still rely on light. If ink swallows the signal, the algorithm invents or drops readings.

How to get the data back

The Sensor Tattoo Fix lens gives the optical sensor a readable path again — without changing brands or removing the tattoo.
