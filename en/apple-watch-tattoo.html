Apple Watch + tattoo: constant passcode and missing heart rate

On Apple Watch, ink under the sensor often shows up as endless unlock prompts, broken heart rate, or workouts that never finish — the watch thinks it left your wrist.

Typical Apple Watch behavior

  • Passcode / unlock prompts every few minutes
  • Heart rate dropping out during workouts
  • Wrist detection failing even with a snug band
  • Apple Pay and all-day use becoming frustrating

Why

Apple’s optical sensor needs reflected light. Dark ink under the contact point weakens the signal; security treats that as “off wrist” and locks.
