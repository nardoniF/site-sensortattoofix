What you may notice on Apple Watch

  • Repeated passcode or unlock prompts during the day
  • Wrist detection failures (the watch thinks it left your arm)
  • Interrupted or gappy heart-rate readings
  • Features that rely on wrist detection feeling less stable

Band fit, motion, sweat, and uneven contact also matter. A tattoo is an additional optical factor when ink sits directly under the sensor.

Why a tattoo can interfere

On Apple Watch, the optical sensor and wrist detection rely on light reflected from the skin. Dark ink under the sensor can weaken that signal, so the system may treat the watch as off-wrist and ask for a passcode more often.

Not every tattoo causes this, and different Series or Ultra models can behave differently. A loose band, motion, and sweat also matter — ink is an extra factor, not the only explanation.
