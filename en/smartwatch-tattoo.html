Is your tattoo blocking the smartwatch heart-rate sensor?

If the watch loses your pulse, invents heart-rate spikes, or pauses workouts on a tattooed wrist, the watch often isn’t broken — dark ink is absorbing the sensor light.

Common signs

  • Heart-rate gaps or wild spikes
  • Workouts that pause on their own
  • Passcode / “off-wrist” unlocks all day
  • Sleep, SpO₂ or stress looking wrong

Why tattoos interfere

Optical sensors shine light into the skin and read what comes back. Dense dark ink — especially black under the sensor — soaks up that light, so the watch thinks there’s no pulse.

What usually doesn’t fix it

  • Cinching the band painfully tight
  • Switching wrists if both have ink in the sensor zone
  • Re-calibrating without changing the optics

What works

The Sensor Tattoo Fix lens sits between the sensor and the skin, redirecting light to readable tissue so heart rate, sleep and workouts can work again — without removing the tattoo.
